Yuanqi Du @YuanqiD · 3/ why basins, why it is hard 270
Why is this hard? Basins are separated by barriers of many k_BT, so escape times grow like exp(ΔF/k_BT). MD steps are femtoseconds; the events take µs to hours. Simulations spend almost all their time inside basins and rarely see the crossings that set the kinetics. ⏳
Yuanqi Du @YuanqiD · 4/ the committor 265
The object that captures a transition is the committor q(x) = P_x(τ_B < τ_A): the probability of reaching the product before the reactant. Its q = ½ surface is the transition-state ensemble, and through transition path theory it gives reactive fluxes and rates. 🧭
Yuanqi Du @YuanqiD · 5/ REACT recap: stochastic optimal control 278
Recap of REACT 🎛️: add a control u to the Langevin dynamics. The control that sends every trajectory to B before A is u* = σᵀ∇log q (Doob's h-transform). So q is a value function, learning to steer = learning q, and steered paths sample exactly the region q is uncertain about.
